## About

I am an MSBA graduate from the **University of Washington** with a focus on **analytics**, **decision science**, and **GenAI**. This hub links to interactive Streamlit applications I have built across forecasting, experimentation, pricing, retention, data quality, and LLM-powered workflows.

Each app is meant to be explored hands-on: adjust assumptions, run scenarios, and see how methods behave on realistic problem framings.

### What you will find here

- **Forecasting & revenue** — demand and LTV-style problems, media mix, and revenue planning workflows  
- **Pricing projects** — dynamic pricing and elasticity simulations, conjoint analysis, price–volume–mix (PVM) diagnostics, and LTV/CAC payback framing  
- **Experimentation & growth** — A/B testing, churn risk, and CRM retention impact  
- **Market intelligence** — competitive mapping and benchmark-style positioning views  
- **Data systems** — recommendations, SQL-centric data quality (GuardianSQL)  
- **GenAI** — retrieval-augmented generation over documents and practical tooling such as resume tailoring
